Join the Band

Always wanted to play the bagpipes or the drums? We teach new pipers and drummers of any age, for free, from players who compete with top Grade 1 bands. No experience needed.

  • groups
    All ages welcome Several young students have gone on to become top-rated soloists at Winter Storm.
  • flight_land
    Moving to Kansas City? Experienced players are welcome, and we'll help you connect with the local piping community.
